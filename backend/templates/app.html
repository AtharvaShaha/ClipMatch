Tips for Better Matching
Index your full reference videos first in the Library tab
Clips with captions or borders may have lower confidence scores
Even low confidence matches (10-30%) can indicate a real match
The system uses perceptual hashing - works even with quality changes
Known Limitations
⚠ Mirrored or flipped videos are not detected — hashes are orientation-dependent
⚠ Heavy cropping may reduce match accuracy significantly
⚠ Processing time scales with reference library size (~2s per indexed video)
⚠ Speed-changed clips (sped up / slowed down) may break temporal matching
ℹ Only visual matching is used — no audio fingerprinting
